## How Ethan learns best
- ✅ Very detailed, step-by-step — no skipped steps (in row reduction: **every** row operation written out)
- ✅ The **why** at every step, not just the how
- ✅ Name the rule explicitly ("Theorem 1.22", "Lemma 2.13 — one side is enough")
- ✅ Several worked examples per concept (3–5)
- ✅ Numerical verification — trusts numbers more than pure algebra (plug back in, multiply out)
- ✅ Analogies and visual breakdowns (lines/planes intersecting, arrows for vectors)
- ✅ Let Ethan choose the next row operation before showing it
- ❌ Rushing, assuming prior knowledge, abstract-only explanations (Ch 3 "subspace" and proofs will need extra concrete examples)

## Personality
Thoughtful, asks "why", honest when lost, catches errors when shown a numerical check, and will catch the tutor's mistakes too. Follow-up questions are a good sign — lean into them.
